# The Portkey gateway client. Not needed to make the call — src/gateway.py speaks the same HTTP
# with urllib — but a customer deployment is easier to reason about when the appliance uses the
# vendor's own SDK: their docs, their examples, their retry and timeout defaults.
#
# Two things about it are load-bearing here and were verified before pinning:
#   - a successful turn keeps `hook_results` on the response (r.model_dump() has it), so
#     gateway.extract_scan() reads the AIRS verdict unchanged;
#   - a guardrail block arrives as APIStatusError (446), and the hook results survive on
#     e.response.json(). Reading the verdict off the EXCEPTION is what keeps "was this turn
#     inspected" separate from "did this turn succeed" — see the note at the top of gateway.py.
# A future version that drops either of those breaks the console's scan panel silently, which is
# why this is pinned rather than floating.
portkey-ai==2.3.4
